## Why

Mobile search loses context and starts playback when a result playlist is opened. Desktop search obscures progress and errors, and play-by-row is not keyboard reachable.

## What Changes

- Keep submitted mobile search in navigation, support newer submissions, and browse playlist results before playback.
- Make desktop results and retry states distinct and improve search, filter and track accessibility.

## Impact

- Music browsing views and search service adapter only; no backend, vendor credentials, or playback-engine changes.
