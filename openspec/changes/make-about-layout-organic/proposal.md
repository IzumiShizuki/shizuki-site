## Why

The user finds the current About page too rectangular despite the geometric hero. Continuous bordered panels, equivalent blocks and muddy dark surfaces keep the whole page feeling rigid.

## What Changes

- Delegate the actual layout implementation to Claude, including the full public About composition rather than only its hero.
- Replace repeated framed sections with open typography, fluid imagery, meaningful asymmetry, generous grouping and more natural transitions across biography, interests, life previews, history and writing.
- Coordinate the public side rails and page surfaces with a deliberate dark palette while preserving the day theme.
- Retain real statistics, all current content, editing, links, navigation, loading/error states, responsive layouts and reduced motion.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `organic-about-composition`: A cohesive, less boxed About reading experience with readable dark colors and responsive natural section composition.

### Modified Capabilities

None. The existing public-statistics contract remains unchanged.

## Impact

Public About Vue components and public-workspace-scoped author page styling. No backend/API changes, new dependencies, global theme replacement, deployment or unrelated account work.
