## Why

Glass layered over wallpaper competes with tasks, deadlines and editable controls. The local development branch needs the completed TimePrism appearance ported without importing its independent feature history.

## What Changes

Give the existing Todo and monthly calendar opaque, theme-aware work surfaces with clearer selected states and supporting ink. No changes to controls, data, routes, modules or migrations. The work planner is absent from this target; its original styling remains on `design/timeprism-polish`.
