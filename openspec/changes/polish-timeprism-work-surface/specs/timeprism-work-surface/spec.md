## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Isolated planning work surface
The Todo and calendar work areas SHALL provide opaque, theme-aware reading surfaces and distinguish active and keyboard-focused controls without changing behavior in other applications.

#### Scenario: Read tasks over wallpaper
- **WHEN** a user opens a planning view in the day or night theme
- **THEN** task text, supporting labels and selected controls remain distinguishable independently of the wallpaper
